✨ What is Outstand?

Outstand is a premium productivity platform designed for students, developers, creators, and professionals who want to eliminate distractions and achieve deep work.

Rather than being just another Pomodoro timer, Outstand combines beautiful UI, AAA-quality animations, gamification, and intelligent productivity tools into one seamless experience.

Every interaction is crafted to feel rewarding, keeping users motivated while helping them build consistency and meaningful habits.
