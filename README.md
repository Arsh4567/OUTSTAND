# OUTSTAND (Android)

Turn attention into momentum. An AI-powered productivity system built for Android using Kotlin and Jetpack Compose that turns ambitious goals into daily execution.

## The System: Everything Connects

OUTSTAND guides you from setting a goal to achieving it through consistent daily action:

### 01 · Decide: Turn a Goal into a Route
- **AI Roadmaps:** Give OUTSTAND a goal, and it generates a structured pathway with milestones and daily execution blocks so you always know your next useful move.
- **AI Coach:** In-app contextual guidance tailored to your active roadmap, focus history, and habits.

### 02 · Execute: Focus on the Work
- **Focus Timer:** Distraction-free deep work and sprint sessions with ambient audio modes. Earn 2 XP for every focused minute.
- **Habits & Tasks:** Build repeatable routines, complete meaningful actions, and keep your day moving forward.

### 03 · Compound: Make Progress Visible
- **XP & Levels:** Persistent local progress rewarding real execution.
- **Streaks & Momentum:** See your consistency compound over time with daily streaks.
- **Brain State Calibration:** Track mental energy to align work intensity with cognitive state.

## Tech Stack

- **Language:** Kotlin 2.1.0
- **UI Framework:** Jetpack Compose with Material Design 3 (M3)
- **Local Persistence:** Room Database (SQLite) with KSP
- **Architecture:** MVVM with StateFlow and Coroutines
- **Navigation:** Navigation Compose
- **Design:** Edge-to-edge, adaptive layouts, dark cyber-clean palette
